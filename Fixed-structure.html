Goose Hollow Inn:

A charming eatery off the beaten path. Known for their fantastic ruben.

The Cheerful Bullpen:

A classic dive bar located right across the street from Providence Park. This is a great place to grab a bite or a drink before watching a Timbers or Thorns match.

The Mox Boarding House:

A combination restaurant and board game store. Perfect for meeting some friends and finding new game night activities.
